A corner
shop, plainly

Tucked along Strutt Street in Normanton, Akbar & Sons keeps the kind of shelves a neighbourhood actually needs — bread for the morning, milk for the tea, a tin of something for tea-time, the odd luxury for the weekend.

No rotating banner of "deals". No list of every brand on the shelf. Just a door that opens onto the street and a counter run by the family the name on the front belongs to. This page exists because the shop does — a quiet mark on the internet that points back to a real place in Derby DE23, where the lights are on most days and the basics are where they have always been.
